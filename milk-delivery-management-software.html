Why Rounds Go Wrong

A Milk Round Fails in Small, Expensive Ways

None of these feel like a crisis on the day. Together they cost a dairy a working week every month.

The wrong quantity leaves the dairy

Loading is done from memory or last month's notebook. Some days a rider carries 18 extra litres that come back unsold; other days three houses are missed entirely and you hear about it by phone.

Pauses never reach the rider

A customer messages you at 10pm that they are travelling for a week. It is remembered for two days and forgotten on the third — so you deliver milk nobody wanted and argue about it at month end.

Nobody can prove what was delivered

When a customer disputes four litres on a bill, there is no record to point at. Most dairies simply give the credit, because arguing costs more than the milk.

Cash collected on the route goes untracked

Riders collect cash at the door. What reaches the dairy is reconciled from memory, and shortfalls surface weeks later with no way to trace them.

A new rider needs a week of hand-holding

Routes live in one person's head. When they take leave, the replacement gets a scribbled list and the day turns into a series of phone calls.

The dairy owner is the routing system

Every change, substitution and complaint routes through the owner's phone, during the two hours of the morning when they can least afford it.

A Delivery Day

What Actually Changes Between 4am and 9am

The same round, run on LactoSync.

4:30am — loading

The rider opens the app and sees today's manifest for their route: 214 litres cow, 63 litres buffalo, 22 packets of curd. Two customers on pause are already subtracted; one customer who asked for an extra litre today is already added. The vehicle is loaded once, correctly.

5:00am — the round

The route sheet opens in delivery order. At each stop the rider taps delivered, or marks a skip with a reason. If a house wants half a litre less today, it is changed at the door and the bill reflects it automatically. No signal is required — entries queue on the phone.

7:15am — collection

Three customers pay in cash. The rider records each payment against the customer as it is handed over. The dairy owner sees the running collection total without making a call.

8:00am — close of shift

The route closes with a summary: delivered, skipped, litres out, litres returned, cash collected. Anything unusual is visible immediately, not at month end.

Month end — no reconstruction

Because every delivery was recorded on the day it happened, bills are generated from the actual record. There is nothing to reconstruct, and nothing to argue about.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is milk delivery management software?
Milk delivery management software plans and records daily door-to-door milk rounds. It works out exactly how much milk each route needs, gives every rider an ordered delivery list, lets them mark each stop as delivered or skipped, records cash collected at the door, and feeds all of that into monthly billing automatically. LactoSync does this for dairies across India, with an app that keeps working when the phone has no signal.
Does the delivery app work without an internet connection?
Yes. Riders can open their route and mark deliveries completely offline, which matters in basements, narrow lanes and low-network areas. Entries are stored on the phone and sync automatically the moment the connection returns, so nothing is lost and nothing has to be re-entered.
How does LactoSync handle customers who pause delivery while travelling?
A pause is set once, with a start and end date. From that moment the customer is removed from the loading manifest and the route sheet for those days, and added back automatically when the pause ends. Because the pause flows straight through to billing, the customer is never charged for days they did not receive milk.
Can we track how much cash each delivery rider collected?
Yes. Riders record payments against the customer at the doorstep, so collection is tracked per rider and per route as it happens. At the end of a shift you see exactly what was collected and what is still outstanding, instead of reconciling from memory at the dairy.
Can one dairy run multiple routes and multiple riders?
Yes. Customers are assigned to routes and shifts, and each rider sees only their own route for that shift. Owners see every route in one view, with live progress across all of them. Adding a route or moving customers between routes takes a few seconds.
What happens when a customer wants extra milk on a particular day?
A one-off change can be made in advance by the office or at the door by the rider. The extra quantity is added to that day's loading manifest, recorded against the delivery, and picked up automatically in the month's bill — no separate note, no adjustment at month end.
How long does it take to move our existing delivery rounds into LactoSync?
Most dairies are running within a day. Customers, rates, routes and shifts are set up during a guided session — we can import an existing customer list rather than typing it in — and riders usually need one round to get comfortable with the app.
