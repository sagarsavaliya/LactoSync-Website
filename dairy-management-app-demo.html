What You'll See

Everything a Milk Dairy Runs On, in One App

Six everyday jobs the LactoSync owner app takes off your plate

0:03

Your dairy at a glance

The home screen shows today's milk requirement for the morning and evening shifts, split by product and by bottle or bag size, so you know exactly how many litres to prepare. Every customer is one search away.

0:09

Every customer's month on one screen

Open a customer to see their subscription, rate, delivery shift and a calendar of what was delivered each day. This month's bill and any pending dues sit right below, with Record payment and Remind buttons.

0:17

Daily orders and delivery routes

Adjust today's quantities in a tap, then drag stops into delivery order for each rider. The route summary shows exactly how much of each product to load, by container size.

0:30

Milk bills that build themselves

Monthly bills come straight from the delivery log, so there is nothing to calculate by hand. Each invoice carries a day-by-day order calendar your customer can check, plus previous dues and the total payable.

0:37

Payments in, receipts out

Record a cash, UPI or bank transfer payment against the customer's bill. The balance updates instantly and the customer receives a payment receipt on their phone.

0:45

No more chasing dues

The Overdue payments screen groups customers by how many months they owe. Send reminders to all of them in one tap, or write a notice, such as a milk rate change, for every customer or a saved list.

Questions

Getting Started With LactoSync

How do I start using LactoSync for my dairy?

Register your dairy at dairyfarm.lactosync.com with your dairy name and mobile number. We verify your number on WhatsApp, you set a PIN, and you can start adding customers. You can also import your customer list from Excel or CSV.

Does it work for my delivery boys and my customers too?

Yes. LactoSync has three connected apps: the owner app shown in this video, a delivery rider app with pickup manifests and route stops that works without internet, and a customer app where customers see their deliveries, bills and payments.

Which languages does the app support?

English, Hindi and Gujarati. This video also has Hindi and Gujarati subtitles; choose them from the CC menu in the player.

How much does LactoSync cost?

Plans start at ₹499 per month plus GST for up to 200 customers, with flat pricing and no per-delivery charges. See the full pricing table.
