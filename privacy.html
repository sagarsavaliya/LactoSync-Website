This policy is written in plain language wherever possible. It applies to the LactoSync mobile app (owner, delivery staff, and customer experiences), the LactoSync admin web dashboard, and this website (lactosync.com). It does not constitute legal advice — if you have a specific compliance question, please consult a qualified professional.

1. Who we are

LactoSync is a dairy farm management platform built and operated by Akshara Technologies ("LactoSync", "we", "us", or "our"). LactoSync helps dairy farm owners manage customers, delivery routes, billing, and milk production, and gives their customers and delivery staff dedicated app experiences.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy explains what personal data we collect from three categories of users — Farm Owners (who register and administer a dairy farm account), Delivery Staff (added by a Farm Owner to run delivery routes), and Customers (added by a Farm Owner as households receiving deliveries) — why we collect it, and what choices you have.

Farm Owners act as the data controller for the customer and delivery-staff records they add to their account (names, addresses, order history, etc.), since that data belongs to their business relationship with their own customers and staff. LactoSync acts as the data processor for that farm-level data, and as the data controller for account and platform-level data (login credentials, subscription/billing records, app usage).

3. Data we collect

3.1 Account & identity data

  • Full name, mobile number, and role (owner / delivery staff / customer)
  • Farm name, business address, and GST / tax details for Farm Owners (such as GSTIN, legal/trade name, and billing address) when provided for subscription invoicing or farm profile setup
  • A hashed PIN used for app sign-in (we never store your PIN in plain text)
  • Profile photo, if you choose to upload one

3.2 Operational data

  • Customer subscription details: products, quantities, delivery schedule, vacation/skip dates
  • Delivery records: route assignment, delivery status, timestamps
  • Billing records: invoices, payment mode, payment status, outstanding dues
  • Milk-preparation and product-catalog data entered by the farm owner
  • Communication logs for WhatsApp notifications sent through the platform (bill, reminder, delivery-update, and vacation messages) — message content and delivery status, not your private WhatsApp conversations

3.3 Subscription billing & GST data (Farm Owners)

When you subscribe to a paid LactoSync plan, we collect and process the information needed to raise GST-compliant tax invoices for our subscription fees, which may include:

  • Billing name, billing address, and state of supply
  • GSTIN (if you are registered under GST and provide it)
  • Plan selected, plan price (exclusive of GST), GST rate/amount (currently 18%), and total payable
  • Invoice number, invoice date, payment status, and related tax invoice / credit note records

Providing a GSTIN is optional if you are not GST-registered. If you do not provide a GSTIN, we may still charge applicable GST on the subscription and issue a tax invoice / bill of supply as required under Indian GST law, based on the billing details you share.

3.4 Payment data

We record which payment mode was used (cash, UPI, bank transfer) and the amount/date of a payment, so that billing history stays accurate. We do not collect or store full card numbers, UPI PINs, or net-banking credentials — payments made online are processed directly by the relevant payment gateway or UPI app, which has its own privacy policy.

3.5 Device & usage data

  • Device type, operating system, and app version, for crash diagnostics and compatibility
  • Push-notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging), so we can deliver in-app alerts
  • Basic in-app activity (pages visited, actions taken) to improve reliability and performance

3.6 Website data

If you fill out the "Schedule a Demo" form on this website, we collect the name, phone number, email address, and any farm details you choose to provide, solely to respond to your enquiry.

4. How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account, and authenticate you at sign-in
  • To operate core features: customer directories, delivery routes, billing, and milk-prep tracking
  • To bill Farm Owners for LactoSync subscriptions, calculate and charge applicable GST, and issue GST-compliant tax invoices / credit notes
  • To maintain tax, accounting, and audit records required under Indian law (including GST)
  • To send transactional notifications (bill ready, payment reminder, delivery update, vacation confirmation, OTP) via WhatsApp, SMS, or push notification
  • To respond to support requests and demo enquiries
  • To maintain an audit/activity log so farm owners can see who changed what, and restore accidental changes
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents
  • To improve app reliability and performance through aggregated, non-identifying usage analysis

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data to third parties for their own marketing purposes. We do not use customer delivery data for advertising.

5. GST & tax invoicing

LactoSync subscription fees are exclusive of GST. Applicable GST (currently 18%) is charged in addition and shown on pricing pages and on every invoice. See our Pricing page and Terms of Service — Pricing & billing for current plan amounts.

  • Our invoices to you: Akshara Technologies issues tax invoices for LactoSync subscription fees. GSTIN and billing details you provide are used only to prepare those invoices correctly and to meet GST / tax compliance obligations.
  • Your invoices to your dairy customers: If you use LactoSync to generate bills for your own milk customers, you remain the seller and tax-compliance party for those supplies. LactoSync is a record-keeping and billing tool; we do not collect GST on your behalf from your customers, and we are not responsible for your GST registration, returns, or tax liability on milk/dairy sales.
  • Accuracy: You are responsible for ensuring the GSTIN, legal name, and billing address you provide to us are accurate. Incorrect GST details may result in invoices that cannot be claimed for input tax credit.
  • Disclosure to authorities: We may disclose invoice, GSTIN, and related billing records to tax authorities, auditors, or other competent authorities where required by law (for example, GST returns, assessments, or lawful requests).

6. Legal basis for processing

We process personal data on the basis of: performance of the contract between a Farm Owner and their customers/staff (delivering the service they signed up for), our legitimate interest in operating and securing the platform, your consent where required (for example, WhatsApp notification opt-in), and compliance with applicable law — including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), and tax/GST laws that require us to issue invoices and retain financial records.

7. Who we share data with

We share data only where necessary to operate the service:

  • WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta): to deliver bill, reminder, and delivery notifications you or your farm owner has opted into
  • Cloud hosting & infrastructure providers: our application and database run on servers located in India
  • Push notification service (Firebase/Google): to deliver in-app alerts
  • Payment gateways / UPI providers: only when you make an online payment directly through them
  • Tax, accounting & compliance providers: invoice and GST-related billing data as needed to prepare GST returns, maintain books of account, or respond to statutory requirements
  • Law enforcement, tax authorities, or regulators: only where required by a valid legal request or applicable tax law

Every third party we work with is contractually required to protect your data and use it only for the purpose we specify (except where a regulator or court requires disclosure).

8. Data security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) between the app and our servers, hashed storage of PINs and passwords, role-based access control so staff only see what their role requires, and daily automated backups. No system is 100% secure, and we encourage you to use a unique PIN and keep your device secure. If we become aware of a data breach affecting your personal data, we will notify affected users and relevant authorities as required by applicable law.

9. Data retention

We retain account and operational data for as long as the farm account remains active, plus a reasonable period afterward to comply with tax, accounting, and legal obligations. GST tax invoices, credit notes, and related subscription billing records are typically retained for at least 7 years (or longer if required under the CGST / SGST / IGST Acts or other Indian statutory requirements), even after an account is closed. Other personal data is deleted or anonymized within a reasonable period after account closure, except where retention is required by law.

10. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data (including GSTIN and billing details used on invoices)
  • Request deletion of your data, where it is not required for legal or contractual reasons (tax/GST invoice records that we must keep by law cannot be fully deleted until the statutory retention period ends)
  • Withdraw consent for optional processing (e.g., WhatsApp notifications) at any time
  • Export your data in a portable format (Farm Owners can export customer/billing data to CSV/Excel directly from the app)
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority

Customers and delivery staff whose data is added by a Farm Owner should first raise access or deletion requests with that Farm Owner, since they control the underlying business relationship. If you're unable to resolve it with them, contact us directly using the details below and we will assist.

11. Children's privacy

LactoSync is intended for business use by adults (dairy farm owners, their staff, and adult customers). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18. If you believe a child's data has been provided to us without appropriate consent, contact us and we will remove it.

12. Cookies & tracking

This website uses minimal, functional cookies/local storage only — for example, to remember your form progress. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log basic request data (such as IP address) as part of standard web infrastructure.

13. International data transfers

Your data is primarily stored and processed on servers located in India. Where we use third-party services (such as Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform or Google Firebase) that process data outside India, we rely on those providers' standard data-protection safeguards.

14. Third-party links & services

Our website and app may contain links to third-party services (WhatsApp, payment gateways, app stores). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties — please review their own privacy policies.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will update the "Last updated" date above, and for material changes, we will notify Farm Owners via the app or email. Continued use of LactoSync after a change means you accept the updated policy.
