Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about LactoSync

General Questions

What is LactoSync?
LactoSync is a comprehensive dairy farm management software that automates your entire operations - from customer management and billing to delivery tracking and milk production monitoring. It's designed specifically for dairy farms in India.
Who can use LactoSync?
LactoSync is perfect for dairy farms of all sizes - from small family businesses serving 20 customers to large cooperatives with 500+ customers. Whether you deliver milk door-to-door or run a dairy shop, LactoSync can help.
What devices does LactoSync work on?
LactoSync works on smartphones (Android & iOS), tablets, laptops, and desktop computers. The mobile app is perfect for delivery boys, while the web dashboard is ideal for managers and owners.
Do I need internet connection?
The mobile app works offline! Delivery boys can mark deliveries even without internet, and data syncs automatically when they're back online. The web dashboard requires internet.

Pricing & Billing

How does the pricing work?
Simple, flat monthly pricing based on your customer count — no per-delivery or per-message charges to track. Plan prices exclude GST; 18% GST is added at billing:

• Basic — ₹499/month + GST ₹89.82 = ₹588.82 total (up to 200 customers)
• Starter — ₹699/month + GST ₹125.82 = ₹824.82 total (up to 300 customers)
• Growth — ₹899/month + GST ₹161.82 = ₹1,060.82 total (up to 400 customers)
• Pro — ₹1,099/month + GST ₹197.82 = ₹1,296.82 total (up to 500 customers)

Running more than 500 customers? We'll put together a custom Enterprise plan (GST applies there too).
Is GST included in the plan price?
No. The listed plan prices (₹499, ₹699, ₹899, ₹1,099) do not include GST. An additional 18% GST is charged on every invoice. The pricing table on this page shows the plan price, the GST amount, and the exact total you pay — nothing is hidden at checkout.
Can I try LactoSync before committing?
Yes — book a free guided walkthrough with our team and we'll set up a demo account using your own farm details, no credit card or commitment required.
What happens if I outgrow my plan?
You can upgrade to the next tier at any time as your customer count grows — just reach out to our team and we'll move you over before you hit your plan's limit.
Are there any hidden charges?
No. Your monthly plan price is fixed and covers every feature listed for that tier — no setup fees or surprise costs. The only addition to the listed plan price is 18% GST, which is shown upfront in the pricing table and on your invoice. WhatsApp message charges (where applicable) are billed separately by Meta at cost (roughly ₹0.10–0.20 per message).
Can I get a discount for large volumes?
Yes! For dairy farms with 500+ customers, we offer custom Enterprise plans with volume pricing. Contact our sales team for a personalized quote.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, and bank transfers. Invoices are sent monthly, and payments are due within 7 days.

Features & Functionality

Can I import my existing customer data?
Yes! You can easily import your customer list from Excel/CSV files. We also provide step-by-step guidance and support to help you migrate your data.
Does it support WhatsApp notifications?
Yes! LactoSync integrates with WhatsApp to send automated payment reminders, bills, delivery updates, and other notifications to your customers. This feature is available in paid plans.
Can delivery boys use it without training?
Absolutely! The mobile app is extremely simple and intuitive. Most delivery boys learn it in 5 minutes. We also provide video tutorials in Hindi and Gujarati.
Can I track multiple dairy farms?
Yes! If you own multiple dairy farms, each farm gets its own separate account with independent customer lists, billing, and reports.
Does it work in regional languages?
Currently, LactoSync supports English, Hindi, and Gujarati. We're working on adding more regional languages based on user demand.

Setup & Support

How long does setup take?
Setup is incredibly fast! You can create your account and add customers in under 30 minutes. Most dairy farms are fully operational within a day.
Do you provide training?
Yes! We provide free video tutorials, documentation, and email support. Enterprise customers also get on-site training and dedicated account managers.
What kind of support do you offer?
Free plans get email support (response within 24 hours). Paid plans get priority email and WhatsApp support (response within 4 hours). Enterprise customers get 24/7 phone support.
Is my data safe and secure?
Absolutely! We use bank-level encryption to protect your data. Daily automatic backups ensure you never lose information. Your data is stored in secure cloud servers in India.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes! There are no long-term contracts. You can cancel your subscription anytime. If you cancel, you'll retain access until the end of your paid period. You can export all your data before canceling.

Technical Questions

What if my phone/computer crashes?
No worries! All your data is safely stored in the cloud. Simply login from any device and continue working. Nothing is lost.
Do I need to buy any hardware?
No! LactoSync works on devices you already own - smartphones, tablets, laptops. You don't need to purchase any special hardware or equipment.
Can multiple people use it simultaneously?
Yes! Unlimited users can access LactoSync at the same time. Set up separate accounts for your manager, delivery boys, accountant, etc., each with appropriate access levels.
Does it integrate with accounting software?
You can export data to Excel/CSV format which can be imported into most accounting software like Tally, QuickBooks, etc. Direct integrations are planned for future updates.
